Founder

Prince Shuro

Founder & Lead Mentor

Prince P. Shuro is a youth advocate, social media content creator and founder of His Safe Space, a youth-led initiative advancing young men’s mental wellbeing through conversation, emotional literacy and community engagement.


His leadership journey began during his tenure as Junior Mayor for the City of Masvingo Junior Council 2022-2023, where he championed youth participation and community development initiatives. Today, he continues that commitment through His Safe Space, leveraging digital storytelling, advocacy and youth-led programming to challenge stigma, promote healthier expressions of masculinity and create spaces where young men feel seen, heard and supported.


Passionate about using influence with responsibility, Prince is dedicated to building a generation of young men who understand that seeking support and expressing emotions are not signs of weakness, but acts of strength.

Vision

To build a lasting community where emotional strength is celebrated and brotherhood is grounded in empathy.

Mission

To make mental health conversations accessible, meaningful, and supportive for every young man who needs them.

The Story

What guides Prince in every part of this work.

Deep Listening

We believe most young men do not need fixing before they need understanding. Every meaningful conversation begins with listening beyond the words; creating space for honesty, reflection and connection.

Redifining Strength

For generations, strength has been measured by silence. We challenge that idea. We believe courage is the willingness to acknowledge struggle, seek support and remain human in a culture that often rewards performance over wellbeing.

Healing Through Community

Mental wellbeing is rarely built in isolation. When young men share experiences, stories and challenges, healing becomes collective. Connection reminds us that what feels personal is often deeply shared.

Prince’s guiding message

“We are raising a generation of young men who know how to perform but not always how to process. My work is rooted in a simple belief: young men deserve spaces where they can be honest before they are expected to be strong. His Safe Space exists to foster those conversations, build meaningful community and redefine what strength looks like in today’s world.”
